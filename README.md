# Gen1 Seed - Latent Geometry Analysis

## Overview
This application visualizes the "Latent Geometry" of a geometric seed. It computes how lines generated by the seed intersect with each other to form new "Latent Points", and how these points relate to the original "Atomic Points".

## Algorithm Rules

### 1. Data Separation (Atomic vs. Latent)
- **Atomic Spectrum**:
  - Based strictly on the original 32 points defined by the seed construction.
  - Ratios are distances between Atomic points.
  - Designated as lines `P1-P2`, `C1-C3`, etc.
- **Latent Spectrum**:
  - Based strictly on intersections involving **Latent Lines**.
  - A **Latent Line** is a line connecting two Atomic points that share a specific ratio.
  - **Latent Points** are intersections of:
    - **Latent x Latent** (Two latent lines intersecting) -> `LatentLatent` Kind.
    - **Latent x Atomic** (Latent line intersecting construction line) -> `LatentAtomic` Kind.
  - **Note**: Intersections with Circles (LCAX) are **EXCLUDED** as they are not linearly measurable.

### 2. Ratio Classification (`LatentRatioType`)
Every distance ratio in the Latent Spectrum is classified into one of three types:

1.  **Pure Atomic**:
    - The ratio exists ONLY between Atomic points that lie on valid construction lines.
    - These are filtered out of the Latent Spectrum view by default.
2.  **Pure Latent**:
    - The ratio exists ONLY between Latent points (or Latent-Atomic pairs not found in Atomic spectrum).
    - It does NOT appear in the Atomic Spectrum.
3.  **Mixed (L/A)**:
    - The ratio appears in *both* the Latent and Atomic spectra.
    - Example: `Mixed (3L/8A)` means the ratio appears 3 times in latent context and 8 times in atomic context.

### 3. Visualization Rules
- **Points**:
  - **Atomic Points**: Red dots (Standard).
  - **Latent-Latent Points**: Magenta dots (Toggle: `Show Latent-Latent`).
  - **Latent-Atomic Points**: Orange dots (Toggle: `Show Latent-Atomic`).
- **Lines**:
  - **Atomic-Atomic**: Cyan/Teal (Standard).
  - **Latent-Atomic**: Cyan/Teal (Standard).
  - **Latent-Latent**: Purple (Distinct color).
- **Selection**:
  - Clicking a Latent Point highlights its **Parent Lines** (the two lines that intersected to form it) in Bright Yellow.

### 4. Analysis output
- `gen1_full_data.txt`: Contains strict Atomic Data.
- `gen1_latent_spectrum.txt`: Contains the Latent Spectrum analysis, including the list of new Latent Points and unique Latent Ratios.

## Controls
- **Show Latent Spectrum**: Enables the latent visualization layer.
- **Show Latent-Latent Lines**: Toggles visibility of purely latent connections.
- **Sidebar**: Click a ratio to isolate it.
